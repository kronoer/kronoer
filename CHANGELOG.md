## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-25

First Preview: Kronoer as a trial app, before it comes to the Play Store.

### Added

- Books with a name, a description, and a cover, and Entries of text with up to 20 photos, whose location is removed.
- An Entry date, set by hand or taken from a photo; text styles that show as you type; and saving as you type.
- The Timeline, with every Entry from every Book day by day, search across all Books or in one, and stars.
- The Trash, where deleted Books and Entries wait for a while before they are gone.
- A daily writing reminder, Indonesian and English, and light and dark themes.
- App lock with the phone's own lock, and Locked Books hidden from the rest of the app.
- Export of everything into an Archive, and Import of an Archive.
