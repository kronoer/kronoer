# Code of Conduct

Kronoer is a place to write personal stories, and the people around it should feel as safe as their Entries.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new or not technical.
- Disagree about ideas, not people.
- Respect privacy: never post someone else's personal data, and never ask anyone to share their Entries or Archives.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention.
- Threats, doxxing, or encouraging others to do any of this.

## Scope

This applies in issues, discussions, pull requests, and anywhere you speak for Kronoer.

## Reporting

Email [moezrd.dev@gmail.com](mailto:moezrd.dev@gmail.com). Reports are kept private. The maintainers may edit or remove content, and warn or block anyone who breaks this code.
